Traime

Turn your notes into quizzes.

Pick a PDF and Traime builds a multiple-choice quiz so you can review the material. Everything is generated with Apple Intelligence right on your iPhone or iPad.

What you can do

  • Quizzes from your PDFs. Notes, syllabi, books… Import the document and start studying.
  • Made to measure. Choose from 5 to 100 questions and the difficulty: easy, medium, or hard.
  • Several documents at once. Combine multiple PDFs into a single quiz to review several topics together.
  • Start right away. The quiz opens with the first questions while the rest are generated as you answer.
  • Understand every answer. Ask for an explanation of any question, based on the text of the document itself.
  • Learn from your mistakes. Questions you get wrong are saved so you can review them anytime, and they come back in your next quizzes.
  • Track your progress. See your latest score, your percentage of correct answers, and your daily study streak.

Truly private

Your documents never leave your device. Traime has no servers or accounts, and uses no analytics or advertising. Learn more in the Privacy Policy.

Requirements

An iPhone or iPad that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings.
